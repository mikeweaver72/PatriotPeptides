Peptide testing

Patriot Peptides USA uses independent analytical laboratories to verify the identity, purity, and content of research peptides.

Our testing standard

Independent testing is an important part of the Patriot Peptides USA quality process. Samples are submitted to third-party laboratories for analytical testing before results are presented with the corresponding product.

Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are shown on the product page for each peptide, and every published report is listed below.
